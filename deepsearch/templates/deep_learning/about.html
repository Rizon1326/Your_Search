What is Deep Learning?

Deep learning is a subset of machine learning in artificial intelligence (AI) that has networks capable of learning from data that is unstructured or unlabeled. Also known as deep neural learning or deep neural network.

How Does It Work?

Deep learning uses a layered structure of algorithms called an artificial neural network. The design of such a network is inspired by the biological neural network of the human brain, leading to the term "neural network".

Applications of Deep Learning

  • Autonomous vehicles
  • Image and speech recognition
  • Healthcare diagnostics
  • Natural language processing
