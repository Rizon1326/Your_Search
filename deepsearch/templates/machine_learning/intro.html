Introduction to Machine Learning

Machine Learning (ML) is a branch of artificial intelligence that focuses on building systems that can learn from and make decisions based on data.

It involves algorithms and statistical models to perform tasks without explicit instructions, relying on patterns and inference instead.

ML is widely used in various applications such as recommendation systems, image and speech recognition, and predictive analytics.

By leveraging ML, businesses can gain insights, automate processes, and improve decision-making.
